eVerbary is a certified translation company based in Albuquerque, New Mexico, offering document translation for official use in the United States. They provide human‑translated, certified documents in over 60 languages, with a price of $24.95 per page and typical delivery within 24 hours. Services cover legal, medical, immigration, financial, and business documents, serving law firms, universities, and individuals.

**Areas:** Translation · Certified · Document · API · Services

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for eVerbary: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
